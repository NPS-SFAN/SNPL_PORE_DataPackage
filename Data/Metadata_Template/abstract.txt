The National Park Service (NPS) San Francisco Area Network (SFAN) has been monitoring western snowy plovers (Charadrius nivosus) since the 1970s. Snowy plovers have been selected as a vital sign for monitoring due to their sensitivity to habitat conditions, making them an important benchmark of habitat quality at Point Reyes National Seashore (PORE). Snowy plovers are monitored to determine trends in the estimated breeding population size, distribution, and reproductive success of snowy plovers at known breeding beaches at Point Reyes National Seashore.

This data package contains snowy plover monitoring data collected at Point Reyes National Seashore from 2009 - 2026. It includes the following datasets: Snowy Plover Observations, Banding, Nesting, Predators, Chick Bands, and Event Metadata.
